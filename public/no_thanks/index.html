    How to play

    No Thanks! by Thorsten Gimmler (Amigo), for 3 to 7 players.

    1. The deck is the cards 3 to 35. Nine are removed unseen, so nobody knows which numbers are missing.
    2. Everyone starts with 11 chips (9 with six players, 7 with seven). In the box game chips are kept secret, but here everyone's count is shown.
    3. On your turn, the top card is face up. Either say No thanks! by putting one of your chips on it and passing it to the next player, or take it along with every chip on it.
    4. If you take a card, you turn over the next one and decide again. If you're out of chips, you must take it.
    5. The game ends when the last card is taken.

    Scoring: each card counts its number as points, but a run of consecutive cards only counts its lowest card (27, 28, 29 scores 27). Each chip takes a point off. Lowest score wins; tied players share the win.

Map projector

After my first trip to Asia, I was surprised by how different the flight paths are than I expected. Apparently Hong Kong is less than 15% closer to San Francisco than to New York! I find it hard to see this just from the map, so I vibe-coded this.

Drag the map to pan. Turn the compass to change which way is up, and click it to put north back up.

Drag the globe to spin the whole world under the map.
